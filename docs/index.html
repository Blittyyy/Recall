FAQ

Common questions

  • How do I save something?

    Open the Add tab and paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Amazon, or the web. On iPhone, you can also share directly into Recall from another app using the system Share sheet. Add a note or reminder if you’d like, then tap Save.

  • Why didn’t my reminder appear?

    Make sure notifications are allowed for Recall in iOS Settings → Notifications. Confirm the reminder time hasn’t already passed, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn’t silencing alerts. If you recently reinstalled the app, open Recall once so notification permissions can be refreshed.

  • Can I edit or delete a save?

    Yes. Open any save from Home, Saved, or Reminders to view details. From there you can update notes and reminders, or remove the save entirely. Your changes are automatically saved to your account.

  • How do I delete my account?

    In Recall, go to Profile → Privacy → Delete account and confirm. This permanently removes your account, saves, collections, and reminders. If you can’t open the app, email GetRecall@outlook.com from the address linked to your account.
