Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Recall respects your privacy. Your saves and reminders belong to you — they stay private to your account and are never sold to third parties.

Data we collect

What information Recall collects

To provide the app, we store information you create and need to sign in:

  • Account details — such as your email address and authentication identifiers used to sign in.
  • Your library — saved links, titles, notes, thumbnails, collections, and reminder schedules you create.
  • App preferences — settings that help Recall work the way you expect (for example appearance or notification-related preferences stored with your account or on device).

What we avoid

What information Recall does not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not show advertising based on your saves.
  • We do not send the contents of your notes, titles, or URLs as analytics properties.
  • We do not use session replay or screen recording analytics.
  • Your saves are private to your account — not a public feed.

Authentication

Supabase authentication

Recall uses Supabase to authenticate users and securely store your account data and library. When you sign in (for example with email or Apple), Supabase manages credentials and session tokens so your data stays tied to your account and syncs across your devices.

Access to your saves is restricted to your authenticated account. Recall uses Supabase to authenticate users and store account data.

Analytics

PostHog analytics

Recall uses PostHog to understand how the product is used so we can improve reliability and experience. Our implementation is intentionally limited and privacy-focused:

  • We track a small set of product events (for example signing up, creating a save, setting a reminder, or opening a notification).
  • Event properties are limited to non-sensitive metadata such as platform type or result-count buckets — not URLs, titles, notes, or email addresses.
  • Session replay is disabled.
  • Users are identified with an opaque account ID where needed; analytics identity is reset on sign-out.
Analytics help us improve Recall without collecting the contents of your saved links, notes, or titles.

Reminders

Notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Recall can send local reminders about saves you’ve scheduled to revisit. Notification permission is optional and controlled in iOS Settings.

Reminder timing and related content stay associated with your account so reminders work as you configured them. You can change or remove reminders inside the app at any time, and you can revoke notification access system-wide whenever you like.

Your rights

Data deletion

You can delete your Recall account at any time from Profile → Privacy → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated Recall data.

If you experience any issues deleting your account, contact us at GetRecall@outlook.com.

Contact

Questions about privacy

If you have questions about this policy or how Recall handles your information, reach us at GetRecall@outlook.com.
